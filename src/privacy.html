Privacy Policy — Animare (3D Character Animator)

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Animare ("the app") is a 3D character animation tool available as a web app and as a desktop application distributed on platforms including the Microsoft Store. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

Animare does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. All of your projects, models, and settings are stored locally on your own device and never leave it unless you choose to export or share a file yourself.

What data the app stores, and where

The app saves your work locally, using your browser's or device's built-in storage (such as IndexedDB or the File System Access API) or, in the desktop app, the local file system. This includes:

  • 3D models, characters, and props you import or create
  • Animation clips, poses, and project files
  • App preferences and settings (e.g. UI layout, viewport options)

None of this data is uploaded to any server. The app has no backend and no user accounts — there is nothing to sign in to, and nothing for us to collect.

Files you import or export

When you import a model or export a project, clip, or rendered file, that action happens entirely on your own device. Files you choose to export are saved wherever you tell the app to save them; the app has no way to access, view, or transmit them elsewhere.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. If you install the app through a distribution platform (such as the Microsoft Store, Google Play, or itch.io), that platform's own privacy policy governs the download and update process itself, separately from anything described here.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub repository: github.com/Callum-OP/3D-Character-Animator.
